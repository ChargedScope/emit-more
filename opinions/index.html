Should horse racing be banned? (UK)


No, people often seem to have quite a warped view on animal welfare, evaluating animals value to live based on their cuteness yet still eating animals that are more sentient/conscious than others. There's no reason to turn your cognitively dissonant ideas about animal welfare into laws that hamper the economy and make people's lives less enjoyable. Also there's no guarantee that you will actually be able to fully ban horse racing as regulation is nothing without enforcement and people will still have the urge to gamble on similar things, likely they'll just bet on sports but it's possible they'd go to a dog fighting ring which is arguably less humane if you care about that sort of thing.



Should the Motorway and dual carriageway speed limit be reduced from 70 mph to 60 mph? (UK)


I don't have a very strong opinion on this, whether the speed limit is 60mph or 70mph isn't going to make the biggest difference in the world but I am slightly against it for a few reasons. The logic for advocating for lower speed limits doesn't seem to have an end, you can always justify a cut to the speed limit as it will lead to less deaths but it's obvious that a 30mph blanket speed limit would be unreasonable. motorways are meant to direct traffic off residential streets, lowering the speed limit will push more people into residential areas if the difference isn't that significant when motorways are much better in many ways. If there are people speeding the difference in speed will make it more dangerous than before. That being said I might support a change to 60 mph for the dual carriageways as they are less safe.



Should secondary schools let students keep their phones on them during the school day? (UK)


Yes, but they shouldn't be using them in class as that's detrimental to learning and it's quite hard for teachers to police this 24/7. At the end of the day the decision should be up to the individual school but if it's possible to adapt to students having phones on them then it should be done.



Should divorce be restricted to exceptional circumstances? (UK)


No, marriage is already treated as a lifelong commitment to your partner, the fact that a minority choose to put an end to their marriage doesn't reduce the legitimacy of it. It's also quite a burden to require someone being abused to prove that they're being abused to disconnect them from their abuser.



Should private schools be abolished (UK)


No, abolishing private schools does not get rid of inequality, people from an advantaged background will still be better off regardless of what school they go to. In the UK private schools don't receive state funding and are subject to VAT, which is already quite an aggressive policy as in many other countries they aren't subject to VAT and sometimes still receive public funding. It's important to note that a little bit of inequality is good, it's unfortunate that in the UK if you weren't born into a wealthy background you're unlikely to climb up the ladder but the UK needs people in high skilled positions and they should receive the appropriate education to ensure they can be efficient in their role.
