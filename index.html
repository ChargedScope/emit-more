Climate change activism groups often try to push for degrowth and a reduction in carbon emissions. This often overlooks the incredible benefits that have been provided to societies through technological growth and the abject poverty suffered by communities left out of it.

Emit More is a website that aims to:
  1. Educate the public about the negative consequences caused by economically contractionary policies pushed by climate change activist organizations.
  2. Help people realise the economic benefits of organizations traditionally viewed as environmentally disruptive.
  3. Inform people about how they can spread proposed ideas and policies and effectuate electoral change.
  4. Show the connection between economic growth and real social benefits.
  5. Empower people through knowledge of economy and the climate rather than through guilt and anger.
